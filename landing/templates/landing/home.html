FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Email hello@staymatch.com

How does the roommate matching work?

Our intelligent system analyzes your lifestyle preferences, study habits, and personal traits to match you with compatible roommates, reducing conflicts and improving your living experience.

Are the listings verified and safe?

Yes. To reduce fraud, we incorporate property verification systems and landlord authentication. Verified properties receive trust indicators ensuring authenticity and reliability.

Can I view the accommodation virtually?

Absolutely. Our system supports digital property viewing through images and virtual tours, allowing you to evaluate accommodation remotely before making decisions.

How do community reviews work?

Users can provide reviews regarding security, water availability, electricity reliability, and landlord behavior. This creates transparency and helps future users make informed decisions.

Who can use this platform?

While ideal for students, the platform also caters to workers, young professionals, and national service personnel seeking shared or individual apartments.
